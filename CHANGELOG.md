## 0.1 beta — First playable build

Tag: `v0.1.0-beta` · pre-release

**First playable build of Producer.io** — a weekly music-career game you can play in the browser or install to your phone.

▶️ **Play it:** https://callofguns.github.io/Producer.io/

### What's in it

**The weekly loop**
- Make songs, release them, collect streams, then hit END WEEK
- 100 energy a week, spent on making, releasing, polishing and training

**Making music**
- Hire Musicality, Songwriting and a Studio — better people cost more, and hiring replaces your own stat with theirs
- Songs carry a **Production Rating** and a **Virality** rating
- Making a song doesn't release it: polish either rating first, then release when you're happy
- Buy a marketing campaign before release, from Local ($500) to Global ($875,000)

**Growing as an artist**
- Artist and Business traits from 1 to 100, trained with energy, with the price climbing as they rise
- **Featured artists** — buy a guest verse to lift a song past your own traits
- **Albums** — group songs onto a record where tracks cross-promote each other

**Money**
- **Lifestyle** spending on Food, Fashion, Health and Home, which raises your maximum energy
- **Job board** with weekly contracts for early income, traded against your energy
- Weekly expenses, with your lifestyle auto-downgrading if you can't cover them

**Other**
- Installable as an app (PWA) and plays offline
- Progress saves automatically, with export and import

### Known gaps
Awards, Certifications, Festivals, Label, Merch, the Financials tab, the Relationships and Transportation lifestyle categories, and the Charisma / Video Directing / Leadership traits are drawn in the UI but not built yet.

This is a beta — saves may need resetting as the game changes.
